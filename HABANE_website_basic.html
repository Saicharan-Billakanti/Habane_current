02 — WHO & WHY

BORN IN LEMGO. BUILT FOR EVERYWHERE.

WE ARE NOT
A LUGGAGE BRAND.

HABÄNE was born inside a German university and accelerated through a programme built for founders who move between worlds. We build intelligent travel objects for people who refuse to move normally — obsessives, creatives and builders who need gear that thinks as hard as they do.
